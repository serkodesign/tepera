## Full description (≤4000 chars)

**Tepera isn't another screen-time blocker.**

Instead of bans and guilt counters, the app gently shows your screen-time vs. real-life ratio — and
lets you quickly log what your offline life is actually made of: walks, books, sleep, hobbies.

**What makes it different:**

📊 **Balance on your widget** — an adaptive home-screen widget shows your Online/Offline ratio for
the day, no need to open the app.

⚡ **1-2 taps, then back to life** — no distracting screens. Log the activity, get back to the moment.

🎯 **A smart target, not pressure** — a gentle buffer early in the day (so 15 minutes at 8am doesn't
look like 100% "dependency"), a customizable daily reference point.

🚫 **Exclusion list** — maps, e-books, banking apps don't count as "screen dependency".

🔒 **Fully private** — usage statistics are processed on-device only, never transmitted. No accounts,
no social feed.

Built in a "monastic" style — no badges, no streaks, no competing with yourself. Just a calm look at
your balance.

---

*Tepera is free, with no ads.*
